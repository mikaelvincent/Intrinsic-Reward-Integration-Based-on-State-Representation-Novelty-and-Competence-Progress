## 3. Technical Background

This chapter presents the theoretical and algorithmic foundations of the study. The discussion covers Markov decision processes, the reinforcement learning objective used with intrinsic reward shaping, Proximal Policy Optimization, Generalized Advantage Estimation, intrinsic reward construction, latent dynamics modeling, online latent-space region partitioning, and evaluation metrics. These topics support the GLPE and GLPE (no gate) methods and establish the basis for the methodology and results chapters.
